In the Hebrew Bible, the blessing of a long life is orekh yamim (אורך ימים), length of days. But the text rarely speaks of length alone. It speaks of days that are full, useful, and lived in strength.

As a graduate of the Israel Institute of Biblical Studies, you already know that studying a text and living by it are two different disciplines. We would like to introduce you to something new, in that same spirit.

Caring well for what we are given

Longevity is not about chasing youth. It is about a practical question: how do we reach seventy, eighty, or ninety with our strength, our minds, and our independence intact, so that the years we are given are years we can use?

Much of the science points to ordinary habits: sleep, what we eat, and how we move. Longevity Life Academy teaches these ideas live, and we would like you to see what that looks like.
