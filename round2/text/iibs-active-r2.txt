First, what is longevity?

The Bible calls a long, full life a blessing. A young field of science now studies how to reach it. Longevity is the study of how to stay healthy, strong and independent for as many years as possible. It is not about living forever. It is about reaching seventy, eighty and ninety with your strength and your mind intact. Researchers now know that everyday habits, such as how we eat, sleep, move and handle stress, shape how fast the body ages.
